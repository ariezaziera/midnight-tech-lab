## Owner-input checklist — current content is absent

For each factual item, provide an approval source/reference, permission for public presentation, any confidentiality limits and the exact approved wording/files. Repository approval flags record a decision; they cannot prove the truth of a claim or a license. Do not commit private evidence or confidential drafts merely to populate metadata.

1. **Biography:** preferred display name/pronouns if desired, concise introduction, background/education/experience only as verifiable and shareable, working approach and approved personal interests. 2. **Skills and professional identity:** actual capabilities, tools and discipline labels with appropriate proficiency/context; roles sought or collaboration positioning if owner wants them stated. 3. **Real projects:** public title/slug, summary, category mapping, exact role/contribution, problem, decisions/process, solution, tools, disclosure/ownership permission. Obtain publication permission for client/employer work before ingesting. 5. **Creative services:** confirm offerings actually available, deliverables/exclusions, suitable use case, process and optional related approved work. Pricing, packages, terms and response times only if explicitly supplied/approved. No presumed agency/team or unoffered discipline.

Unblocks a genuine inquiry exit and release readiness; no backend provider needed for approved direct links.
